DATA GOVERNANCE POLICY — OPTUM IRELAND & UK

GDPR Compliance:
All personal data processed by the data platform must comply with
GDPR Article 5 principles — lawfulness, fairness, transparency,
purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation,
integrity and confidentiality. Data subject access requests must
be fulfilled within 30 days. Right to erasure requests must be
processed within 30 days and verified using Snowflake Time Travel
to confirm data removal across all versions.

HIPAA Compliance:
Protected Health Information (PHI) must never be sent to external
APIs or cloud services without a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).
All LLM processing of patient data must use self-hosted models
(Ollama) running within the corporate boundary. PHI must be
encrypted at rest and in transit. Access logs must be maintained
for 6 years.

Data Retention:
Customer data retained for 7 years after last interaction.
Transaction data retained for 10 years for regulatory compliance.
Snowflake Time Travel retention set to 90 days on all PHI tables.
Snowflake Fail-safe provides additional 7 days recovery window.

Data Classification:
Level 1 — Public: Marketing materials, product descriptions
Level 2 — Internal: Business metrics, aggregated reports
Level 3 — Confidential: Customer PII, financial records
Level 4 — Restricted: PHI, payment card data
